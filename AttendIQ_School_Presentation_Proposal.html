Institutional Discipline Without Hardware Costs

Mobile GPS Staff Governance: Zero Gate Terminals Required

School proprietors no longer need to spend millions on expensive biometric gate terminals, RFID cards, or turnstiles that break down during power outages. AttendIQ™ turns every teacher's existing smartphone into a tamper-proof verification device using calibrated campus geofencing.

📍
150m Campus Geofence

The administrator calibrates the exact school campus coordinates with one click. Teachers cannot clock in unless their smartphone GPS confirms they are physically on school grounds (configurable 50m - 250m perimeter).

📱
Hardware Device Lock

On first clock-in, the teacher's phone hardware signature (Canvas, WebGL & Audio fingerprint) is locked to their profile. A single phone cannot clock in multiple staff members, completely eradicating buddy clock-in.

🔢
Confidential Staff PINs

Every form teacher possesses an individual 4-digit security PIN. Principals can bulk-randomize staff PINs at the start of each term with 1 click and print confidential distribution slips for staff pigeonholes.

💡 Why Schools Choose Mobile Geofencing Over Gate Terminals
Zero Capital Expenditure

Gate access terminals create morning congestion at school entrances, require dedicated security guards to operate, and stop working during power cuts or generator changeovers. With AttendIQ, teachers simply walk into their classrooms and clock in on their own phones in 3 seconds. The school saves significant maintenance and hardware acquisition costs.

Eliminate the exhausting end-of-term rush. AttendIQ™ seamlessly merges cumulative daily attendance percentages with the official Nigerian NERDC continuous assessment framework (40% CA + 60% Exam) to generate publication-grade terminal report cards in seconds.

📈
Automated Broadsheet Arithmetic

Teachers input Test 1 (10%), Test 2 (10%), Midterm Project (20%), and Terminal Exam (60%). AttendIQ instantly calculates total scores, subject averages, letter grades (A1 to F9), and class position rankings (1st, 2nd, 3rd) across the entire class arm with zero human mathematical error.

🖨️
1-Click Printable Terminal Report Sheet

Clicking "Print Terminal Report Sheet" renders a crisp, high-resolution official report card formatted to standard A4 size. Complete with the school crest, student biodata, attendance statistics (Times School Opened, Times Present, Times Absent), and affective/psychomotor rating matrices.

✍️
Digital Principal Stamp & Signature

School leadership can upload their official digital stamp and principal signature. Report cards are automatically branded and stamped upon generation, allowing instant PDF export for direct WhatsApp distribution or batch high-speed printing for Speech and Prize-Giving Day.
